## Situation

The platform licence runs to 31 December 2026. Northwind proposed a three-year term in exchange for holding the per-seat price. We want to keep annual terms and add a data-export clause, which the current contract lacks.

## Positions

**Theirs.** Three-year lock-in, price held at 350 per seat, no export clause because "nobody has asked for one". Stated in their proposal of 8 January.

**Ours.** Annual term, willing to accept a modest increase in exchange for the export clause and a service credit if uptime drops below the SLA. The clause matters more than the price.

## Numbers

| | 2025 | 2026 proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Seats | 220 | 240 |
| Price per seat | 350 | 350 |
| Annual total | 77.000 | 84.000 |

The seat count for Q3 2025 is an estimate because the admin console only exports the current quarter. Noted here because it will come up.

## Chronology

- 2026-01-08, Northwind's written proposal received.
- 2026-01-10, internal review. Export clause identified as the priority.

## Next steps

- Legal review of a draft export clause, by 2026-02-15.
- Ask two peer organisations what their Northwind contracts say on term and export.

## Open questions

- Is the three-year term a genuine requirement on their side or an opening position?
- What does the SLA actually promise today? Nobody has read it since 2023.
